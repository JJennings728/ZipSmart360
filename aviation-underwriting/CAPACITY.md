# Capacity

> **Worked example:** [Northstar Air Group — complete synthetic case study](case-study/NORTHSTAR_AIR_GROUP_CASE_STUDY.md)


## Purpose

Show how underwriting decisions depend not only on price, but also on how much limit or line size an insurer is willing to deploy.

## Portfolio concepts

The capacity analysis should include:

- 100% policy limit;
- requested insurer share;
- modeled insurer line;
- maximum preferred line assumption;
- minimum participation threshold;
- lead versus follow role;
- fleet aggregation;
- hull accumulation;
- territory accumulation;
- war-risk accumulation; and
- referral threshold.

## Capacity decision states

- **Within modeled authority**
- **Within modeled authority with conditions**
- **Referral required**
- **Capacity unavailable in the synthetic scenario**

## Historical market-context note

The Marsh Q4 2024 report illustrated that potential market line size varied by risk class and geography and explicitly cautioned that insurer line sizes were estimates only and subject to individual risk appetite.

The portfolio should follow the same discipline: capacity is an underwriting decision variable, not a guaranteed amount.

## Output

A reviewer should be able to see:

- requested participation;
- modeled available participation;
- binding / referral gap;
- rationale; and
- required escalation.
