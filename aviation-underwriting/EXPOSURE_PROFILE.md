# Exposure Profile

## Objective

Create a transparent picture of what is being insured before any pricing or capacity decision is made.

## Fleet-level fields

Each aircraft record should include:

- aircraft identifier;
- aircraft type;
- model / variant;
- year of manufacture;
- in-service / stored / leased status;
- insured hull value;
- agreed value assumption;
- territory / operating geography;
- utilization;
- passenger configuration;
- annual departures;
- maintenance / MRO status;
- ownership / lease status; and
- war-risk geography flags where applicable.

## Portfolio-level fields

The portfolio summary should calculate or display:

- total fleet count;
- in-service aircraft count;
- stored aircraft count;
- total hull TIV;
- average hull value;
- largest single hull value;
- top-5 hull concentration;
- geographic concentration;
- fleet-type concentration;
- annual passengers;
- annual departures;
- requested liability limit; and
- requested insurer participation.

## Exposure questions

The underwriting review should answer:

1. Where is the exposure concentrated?
2. Is any single hull disproportionately large?
3. Are multiple aircraft exposed at the same location?
4. Is the fleet concentrated by aircraft type?
5. Does geography increase hull or liability severity?
6. Are passenger and departure counts increasing?
7. Are stored or leased aircraft treated differently?
8. Are there meaningful war-risk or confiscation concerns?

## Output

A reviewer should be able to understand the physical and operational exposure before seeing any premium calculation.
