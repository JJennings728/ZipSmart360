# Executive Summary

> **Worked example:** [Northstar Air Group — complete synthetic case study](case-study/NORTHSTAR_AIR_GROUP_CASE_STUDY.md)


## Portfolio purpose

This portfolio demonstrates a structured aviation hull-and-liability underwriting workflow using synthetic data and explicit assumptions.

It is designed to show how an underwriter or risk analyst can move from:

**submission → exposure profile → rating factors → pricing → capacity → loss scenarios → reinsurance / war-risk context → underwriting decision**

The portfolio does not represent carrier authority, actuarial pricing, a quotation, or available insurance terms.

## Core underwriting question

**Can the risk be supported at the requested limits and participation, and if so, under what pricing, capacity, subjectivities, and referral conditions?**

## What the portfolio demonstrates

- fleet exposure analysis;
- hull and liability segmentation;
- passenger / departure / geographic exposure;
- loss-history and safety-profile review;
- capacity and line-size analysis;
- premium-component architecture;
- Hull War and AVN52 awareness;
- reinsurance context;
- submission-gap identification;
- underwriting subjectivities;
- explicit referral logic;
- human review; and
- clear separation of fact, assumption, and model output.

## Source discipline

Historical market context is anchored to Marsh's **Aviation insurance market overview — Q4 2024**, published February 2025.

The underwriting case itself remains synthetic and vendor-neutral.

## Portfolio outputs

1. Executive underwriting summary
2. Exposure profile
3. Rating-factor matrix
4. Pricing framework
5. Capacity / participation analysis
6. Loss-scenario analysis
7. Reinsurance / Hull War / AVN52 context
8. Underwriting decision memo
9. Submission gaps and subjectivities
10. Limitations and disclosure
