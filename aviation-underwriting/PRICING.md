# Pricing

> **Worked example:** [Northstar Air Group — complete synthetic case study](case-study/NORTHSTAR_AIR_GROUP_CASE_STUDY.md)


## Purpose

Demonstrate transparent premium-component architecture rather than present a black-box price.

## Premium components

The synthetic pricing framework may include:

- hull all-risks premium;
- liability premium;
- spare-parts premium;
- stored-aircraft component;
- Hull War premium;
- excess AVN52 component;
- other explicitly documented adjustments; and
- subscription share.

## Pricing workflow

1. Establish exposure basis.
2. Apply documented synthetic rate assumptions.
3. Separate hull and liability components.
4. Apply war-risk or special-peril assumptions where modeled.
5. Calculate 100% premium.
6. Calculate insurer share.
7. Compare against requested / prior synthetic pricing.
8. Flag material pricing gaps for human review.

## Rate adequacy view

The portfolio should display:

- requested premium;
- indicated synthetic premium;
- rate change;
- premium change;
- premium by exposure unit;
- premium by fleet segment;
- premium at insurer share; and
- sensitivity to major assumption changes.

## Important limitation

The model is not actuarial.

Rate assumptions are synthetic and intended only to demonstrate underwriting logic and model transparency.
