# Loss Scenarios

## Purpose

Stress the underwriting decision against plausible severity scenarios.

The scenarios are synthetic and are not predictions.

## Scenario library

### Scenario 1 — Major hull total loss

Model:

- largest single aircraft total loss;
- salvage assumption if any;
- hull deductible;
- insurer share;
- war/all-risks classification; and
- premium-to-loss comparison.

### Scenario 2 — Major partial hull loss

Model:

- repair-cost inflation;
- parts availability;
- downtime;
- severity sensitivity; and
- insurer share.

### Scenario 3 — Passenger liability event

Model:

- passenger exposure;
- liability limit;
- severity range;
- jurisdiction / social-inflation sensitivity; and
- insurer share.

### Scenario 4 — Multi-aircraft aggregation

Model:

- airport / hangar aggregation;
- weather / catastrophe event;
- number of aircraft exposed;
- total hull value exposed; and
- accumulation threshold.

### Scenario 5 — War-risk event

Model:

- geographic exposure;
- aircraft value;
- war-risk coverage assumption;
- applicable modeled sublimit; and
- referral requirement.

## Output

For each scenario, display:

- gross loss assumption;
- policy response assumption;
- modeled insurer share;
- premium multiple;
- capital / capacity implication; and
- underwriting action.
