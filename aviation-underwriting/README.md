# Aviation Underwriting Portfolio

**James Jennings | Aviation Risk · Underwriting Analytics · Exposure Modeling · Decision Support**

This portfolio is a synthetic aviation hull-and-liability underwriting case study designed to demonstrate how specialty-risk information can be translated into a structured, reviewable underwriting workflow.

## Business question

How can an aviation submission be evaluated across fleet exposure, hull values, liability structure, pricing components, capacity, underwriting subjectivities, submission gaps, and operational risk before a final underwriting decision is made?

## Portfolio scope

The fictional insured is **Northstar Air Group**. All fleet values, rates, premiums, capacity assumptions, terms, and underwriting outcomes are synthetic training assumptions.

The portfolio demonstrates:

- fleet-level hull exposure analysis;
- in-service versus stored-aircraft treatment;
- hull and liability premium-component architecture;
- war and allied-perils assumptions;
- subscription-market participation;
- capacity allocation;
- underwriting subjectivities;
- missing-information and submission-gap review;
- application-field mapping;
- human-review controls; and
- explicit model limitations.

## Core analytical themes

### Fleet exposure
Aircraft-level values are organized into a reviewable fleet view so total hull exposure, concentration, storage status, and unit-level assumptions can be inspected.

### Hull & liability
The case study separates hull, liability, spare-parts, storage, and war-related components instead of collapsing the entire account into a single premium number.

### Premium & rate analysis
Illustrative pricing logic is used to demonstrate how assumptions flow into a 100% indication and participating-insurer share. It is not actuarial pricing and does not represent available market terms.

### Capacity & underwriting controls
The portfolio documents subscription structure, subjectivities, required information, and referral considerations so decision logic remains visible.
