# Market Context

> **Worked example:** [Northstar Air Group — complete synthetic case study](case-study/NORTHSTAR_AIR_GROUP_CASE_STUDY.md)


## Reference period

**Q4 2024 aviation insurance market context**  
Primary reference: Marsh, *Aviation insurance market overview — Q4 2024*, published February 2025.

This section is historical market context. It should not be presented as the current 2026 market without additional verification.

## Market conditions reflected in the source

The Marsh report described:

- robust insurance and reinsurance capacity;
- increased competition among insurers;
- pressure on premium rates;
- increasing insurer appetite to deploy larger lines in some cases;
- continued scrutiny of individual risk characteristics;
- rising attritional hull repair costs;
- liability loss-cost pressure;
- supply-chain constraints;
- unresolved Russia-Ukraine leasing uncertainty;
- Hull War capacity developments; and
- AVN52 capacity and pricing dynamics.

## Underwriting relevance

The portfolio should therefore avoid treating pricing as a single static rate.

Instead, it should show how market conditions interact with account-specific factors such as:

- loss history;
- safety record;
- aircraft type;
- fleet size and value;
- geographic area of operation;
- hull aggregation;
- passenger / departure exposure;
- liability limit purchased;
- business operation;
- retention; and
- requested participation.

## Portfolio implementation

Historical market context is used to explain **why** the underwriting model evaluates:

- risk differentiation;
- line size;
- capacity;
- premium adequacy;
- loss sensitivity;
- war-risk exposure; and
- referral conditions.

It does not supply live market quotes or current capacity.
