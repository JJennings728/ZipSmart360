# Limitations

> **Worked example:** [Northstar Air Group — complete synthetic case study](case-study/NORTHSTAR_AIR_GROUP_CASE_STUDY.md)


## Synthetic portfolio

All case-study data is synthetic unless explicitly identified as historical public-market context.

## No binding authority

This portfolio does not represent:

- carrier authority;
- delegated underwriting authority;
- a binder;
- a quotation;
- an insurance policy;
- actual available capacity; or
- current market terms.

## No actuarial opinion

Pricing calculations are synthetic examples.

They are not actuarial indications and have not been independently validated.

## Historical source period

Market context drawn from Marsh's Q4 2024 aviation insurance overview reflects that reporting period and publication date.

It must not be represented as current 2026 market conditions without additional research.

## No proprietary market data

The repository should not contain:

- confidential broker submissions;
- proprietary carrier pricing;
- private claims information;
- non-public policy wordings;
- licensed market databases;
- vendor credentials; or
- restricted avionics data.

## Model risk

Outputs are sensitive to:

- source-data quality;
- assumptions;
- loss-history completeness;
- fleet valuation;
- geography;
- pricing inputs;
- policy structure;
- capacity assumptions; and
- scenario design.

## Professional judgment

The model is intended to support structured review, not replace underwriting judgment.
