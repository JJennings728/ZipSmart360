# Underwriting Decision

> **Worked example:** [Northstar Air Group — complete synthetic case study](case-study/NORTHSTAR_AIR_GROUP_CASE_STUDY.md)


## Decision framework

The final output should not be a score alone.

It should state:

**Decision → rationale → conditions → pricing → capacity → missing information → escalation**

## Decision states

### Quote

Use when:

- exposure is understood;
- modeled appetite is satisfied;
- pricing is supportable within portfolio assumptions;
- capacity is available; and
- no unresolved referral item remains.

### Quote with subjectivities

Use when the risk is supportable but binding depends on specified information or conditions.

Examples:

- updated loss runs;
- confirmed fleet values;
- maintenance documentation;
- safety-management information;
- updated geographic schedule;
- war-risk clarification; or
- final capacity approval.

### Refer

Use when:

- requested limits exceed modeled authority;
- fleet value or aggregation exceeds threshold;
- loss experience is adverse;
- geographic or war-risk concerns are material;
- safety information is incomplete;
- pricing falls outside tolerance; or
- reinsurance / capacity support requires escalation.

### Decline

Use only when the synthetic appetite rules identify a clearly unsupported condition.

The rationale must be explicit.

## Required decision memo fields

- insured / synthetic account;
- decision;
- requested limits;
- offered limits;
- requested premium;
- indicated synthetic premium;
- insurer share;
- principal favorable factors;
- principal adverse factors;
- missing information;
- subjectivities;
- referral items;
- final rationale; and
- model limitations.

## Human accountability

No portfolio output should imply autonomous binding authority.

A human reviewer owns the final underwriting decision.
