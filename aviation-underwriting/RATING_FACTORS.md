# Rating Factors

> **Worked example:** [Northstar Air Group — complete synthetic case study](case-study/NORTHSTAR_AIR_GROUP_CASE_STUDY.md)


## Rating-factor framework

The portfolio uses a transparent underwriting-factor matrix.

### Safety and loss history

Review:

- five-year loss history;
- major versus attritional losses;
- loss frequency;
- loss severity;
- safety-management information;
- incident trends; and
- corrective-action evidence.

### Aircraft and fleet characteristics

Review:

- aircraft type;
- aircraft age;
- fleet value;
- fleet size;
- concentration by model;
- spares / engines where relevant; and
- stored-aircraft exposure.

### Geographic exposure

Review:

- operating regions;
- conflict-sensitive territories;
- catastrophe-prone locations;
- airport aggregation;
- sanctions / geopolitical considerations where legally relevant; and
- war-risk accumulation.

### Passenger and operational exposure

Review:

- passenger count;
- departure count;
- stage length / utilization where modeled;
- cargo / passenger mix;
- operating model; and
- route concentration.

### Liability structure

Review:

- combined single limit;
- sublimits;
- self-insured retention;
- deductible;
- passenger liability;
- third-party liability; and
- excess AVN52 structure where applicable.

### Management and controls

Review:

- safety-management system;
- maintenance / MRO controls;
- training and operating procedures;
- cyber resilience where relevant;
- risk mitigation; and
- quality of submission data.

## Rating output

Each factor should be classified as:

- **Favorable**
- **Neutral**
- **Adverse**
- **Referral required**
- **Missing information**

The model should show the reason for the classification rather than only producing a score.
