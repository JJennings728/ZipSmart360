## Proposed prototype
**SRP Compliance Intelligence Engine (CIE)** — in-development, offline evidence triage. It records user-provided source IDs and evidence, flags missing links or human review, and never decides whether a customer complies with law. It does not retrieve updated regulatory text or continuously monitor agencies.
