# Workflow Definition

## Candidate workflow

**Aviation / specialty submission triage and underwriting preparation**

### Current-state pattern to validate in discovery

A submission may contain a broker slip/application, exposure or fleet schedule, loss runs, engineering/risk-control material, CAT or accumulation output, policy wording, facultative reinsurance terms, and supplementary correspondence.

The customer workflow may require analysts or underwriters to manually locate and reconcile relevant information before senior review. This is a **hypothesis until validated with the customer**.

## Proof-of-value target

The reference workflow tests whether AI assistance can help prepare a review package that identifies missing information, summarizes material exposures, highlights loss-history observations, flags FAC/wording questions, separates facts from assumptions, identifies items requiring specialist referral, and leaves the final decision with an authorized human.

## Discovery evidence to capture

| Area | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|
| Customer problem | What business problem is the customer trying to solve? |
| Workflow pain | Where does delay, rework, searching, inconsistency, or manual effort occur? |
| Owner | Who owns the workflow and desired outcome? |
| Affected users | Who performs or receives the work? |
| Impact signal | Time, backlog, rework, errors, service delay, quality, cost, or risk |
| Data | What sources are authoritative and permitted? |
| Systems | What applications or repositories are involved? |
| Governance | Human approval, audit, privacy, security, compliance, retention |
| Success criteria | What would constitute a useful proof of value? |
| Next step | What has the customer actually agreed to do? |

## Example proof-of-value measures

Do not promise improvements before measuring a baseline. Potential measures include time to prepare a summary, percentage of known missing items detected, extraction accuracy against a reviewer-created answer key, percentage of model statements traceable to supplied evidence, reviewer correction rate, number of files/screens manually reviewed, reviewer usefulness score, and percentage of test cases correctly escalated for human review.

## Exit criteria

A proof of value should proceed toward production only when the workflow owner confirms the use case remains valuable, output quality meets agreed criteria, security/governance requirements can be satisfied, the human-review operating model is accepted, integration dependencies are understood, and ownership/support responsibilities are defined.
