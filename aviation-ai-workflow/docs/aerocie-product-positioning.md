# AeroCIE | Product positioning and advertising controls

Public contact: **info@sriskpartners.com**. Brand separators: vertical bar **|**, not forward slashes. Use the supplied original SRP logo and cleared Adobe Stock aviation imagery. Do not reinterpret or reposition the original logo between formats.

## Current product | can be demonstrated internally
**AeroCIE | Aviation Regulatory Evidence Research Prototype**

Source-linked FAA Federal Register collection | metadata-change records | candidate screening | local read-only dashboard | draft research packets | self-attested human review receipts | local candidate research questions (AeroCIE-008).

No live customer implementation, no 24-hour guaranteed monitoring, no proven time savings, no verified regulatory applicability, no legal compliance certification, no enterprise SaaS deployment.

## Product under development | strategic target
**AeroCIE | AI-Native Aerospace Compliance Infrastructure**

Target workflow: **Monitor | Interpret | Map | Review | Document**.

Planned components include candidate obligation mapping, evidence-gap analysis, AI-assisted extraction with structured outputs, user assignments and deadlines, authenticated reviewers, multi-jurisdiction research, client-specific reporting, and hardening.

These are **targets, not available product features**. The local AeroCIE-008 candidate register is not actual regulatory obligations management.
