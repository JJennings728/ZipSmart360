## Recommended research sequence

- Start with AIM's underwriting and operations leaders. They control a specialty aviation underwriting workflow and have an explicitly announced focus on technology and portfolio growth.
- Approach Old Republic's light aircraft product leader to validate the risk factors and MRO observations. Ask about data definitions and underwriter referrals before subscription purchasing.
- Approach SkyWatch, which already markets a technology-driven aviation insurance process, for product discovery and potential integration. It may also be a competitor; do not share proprietary source code or nonpublic metrics in an introductory call.
- Approach regional and senior carrier leaders after a source-verified pilot scorecard exists. Ask for an underwriting operations sponsor, an actuary who can review performance, and an IT procurement contact.
- Speak with reinsurers after testing how the model behaves on real historical insured exposures.

## What to ask during a 20-minute discovery

1. Which repair and parts availability indicators materially affect your quote or referral decisions?
2. What happens when you lack a current MRO observation for an airframe or repair class?
3. What data and manual research are required to justify a quote or referral to a senior underwriter?
4. Which model errors or undocumented assumptions would make the tool unusable?
5. Would a 60-day, $2,500 evaluation with an agreed sample and success metrics warrant an introduction to the responsible budget owner?

## Outreach measurement

Track attempts, delivered messages, replies, meetings, evaluated submissions, offers, paid pilot agreements, and annual conversion as different stages. The proposed 20-30% enterprise conversion and 85% market confidence numbers are hypotheses, not verified AeroCIE benchmarks.
