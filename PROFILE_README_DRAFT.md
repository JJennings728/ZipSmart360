## What I work on

```text
Data
  ↓
Validation
  ↓
Analytics
  ↓
Decision Logic
  ↓
AI / Agents
  ↓
Evaluation
  ↓
Business Action
```

Across projects, I focus on four engineering principles:

- **Traceability** — inputs, assumptions, transformations, and outputs should be inspectable.
- **Validation** — invalid conditions should be identified before they propagate downstream.
- **Domain translation** — business expertise should become explicit rules, schemas, workflows, and software behavior.
- **Human accountability** — AI and analytics should support defensible decisions without hiding uncertainty.
